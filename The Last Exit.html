THE LAST EXIT

You woke up inside a sealed mall. The power is failing and something is hunting you. Restore the generator, find the security keycard, then escape through the shutter in the south-east corner.

The creatures are nearly blind, but they hear everything. Running, gunfire and the generator all carry. Ammo is scarce.

WASD move, Shift run (loud), C crouch (quiet), mouse or arrow keys look, click or Space fire, F flashlight, E interact, 1-3 heal, 4-6 weapons, Esc pauses and opens the survival guide. Desktop keyboard required.

SURVIVOR’S GUIDE

Game paused. You wake up in the mall’s storage room, the best-stocked place in the whole building. Loot the chests here, then head out into the glass-roofed atrium.

Your goal

  • Find and start the generator (the arrow at the top-left points the way).
  • Find the security keycard.
  • Escape through the shutter on the far east wall.

Your explored map. Teal dot: you. Red mark: the east exit.

Controls

  • WASD move, Shift run (loud), C crouch (quiet)
  • Mouse or arrow keys look, click or Space fire
  • F flashlight, E open chests and use things
  • Esc pause and open this book

Healing (like Fortnite)

  • 1 Bandages: +25 health over 5 seconds
  • 2 Medkit: +75 health over 10 seconds
  • 3 Med spray: +50 health slowly over 20 seconds
  • R Painkillers +30, V Adrenaline +50 fast, B Herbal tonic +40, N IV bag +80 slow, T Trauma kit +100

Heals stack, so use several when you are in trouble.

Weapons

  • 4 Pistol: reliable, 1 ammo per shot
  • 5 Shotgun: hits everything in a wide cone, 2 ammo
  • 6 SMG: very fast fire
  • 7 Rifle: strong and steady
  • 8 Sniper: 6 damage, slow reload
  • 0 Magnum, and Q or the mouse wheel cycles every gun you own.
  • Lockers hide 31 guns in total, from silenced pistols to a minigun and a rocket launcher. Z and Q cycle back and forward.

Guns are found in steel weapon lockers.

Chests (press E)

  • Green crate: ammo and batteries
  • White and red case: bandages, medkits, spray
  • Steel locker: guns and ammo
  • Wooden supply crate: healing items and batteries
  • Red toolbox: ammo, trauma kit, adrenaline
  • Black and gold vault: a rare gun, ammo and the best healing

Almost every shop has one, and loose supplies lie on the floors.

Monsters

  • They are almost blind, but they hear running, gunfire and the generator.
  • Zombies roam everywhere. Crawlers and hounds haunt the west, fast runners and wraiths the north-east, and tanks, brutes and tall stalkers hold the south and the atrium. Brutes guard the generator.
  • New ones keep stepping out in front of you, so keep moving.

Districts

NW Toy Land, N Fashion Row, NE Cinema and Arcade, W Electronics, E Food Court, SW Pharmacy (medical supplies), S Sports and Hardware (guns and ammo), SE Supermarket. The atrium is in the centre and the storage room is in its south-west corner.

Tips

Crouch near enemies, switch off your flashlight to hide, and never fire needlessly.
